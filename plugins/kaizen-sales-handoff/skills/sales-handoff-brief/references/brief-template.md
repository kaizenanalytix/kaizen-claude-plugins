# KT Brief Section Layout

Reference for section order, headings, and content expectations.

## Section 1 — Deal Summary
One-row summary table. Columns: Client | Project ID | Start Date | End Date | Delivery Model | TCV | Sales Lead

## Section 2 — Scope & Objectives
Subsections: In-Scope (bullet list ≤10 items) | Out-of-Scope | Key Deliverables | Success Criteria

## Section 3 — Solution Approach
Subsections: Solution Summary (2–3 sentences) | Technologies & Tools | Data Sources & Integrations | Architecture Notes

## Section 4 — Data Requirements
Table: Data Source | Agreed Delivery Date | Access Type | Sensitivity (PII/GDPR/HIPAA/None)

## Section 5 — Key Risks & Assumptions
Table: Item | Type (Risk / Assumption / Dependency / Open Item) | Owner | Status (Open/Resolved)

## Section 6 — Commercial Terms
Table: TCV | Payment Milestones | Billing Structure | Budget Cap
Penalty clause / SLA block below table (yellow highlight if present).

## Section 7 — Team & Contacts
Contact table: Role | Name | Organisation | Email

## Section 8 — Open Items for Delivery Team
Table: Item | Priority (High/Med/Low) | Owner

## Section 9 — Phase 0 Checklist Status
Two sub-tables — Deliverables (D1–D10) and Tasks (T1–T9).
Columns: # | Name | Mandatory | Status (Complete / Not Found in Docs / N/A)

## Section 10 — Recommended Next Steps (Phase 1)
Bullet list of mandatory Phase 1 tasks and deliverables.
